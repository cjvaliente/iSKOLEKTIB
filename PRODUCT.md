# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

UP Mindanao students looking for scholarships and university staff who maintain listings and review student applications.

## Product Purpose

iSKOLEKTIB is a scholarship discovery and application-management prototype. Students can browse and match with opportunities, submit prototype applications, and track their status. Admin staff can publish listings and review applications.

## Positioning

The prototype brings scholarship discovery, eligibility matching, application materials, review status, and disbursement tracking into one student-facing workflow.

## Operating Context

Scholarship listings are based on emails from the UP Mindanao Scholarship and Financial Assistance Section, OSA. The interface serves both students and admin staff through role-specific views.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current app is a demonstration prototype; its records and actions do not integrate with UP systems, providers, or government agencies.
- Student applications and status tracking shown in the prototype are not official submissions or financial records.
- A future UP partnership is possible but is not current product functionality or a present commitment.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the iSKOLEKTIB name and supplied `Iskolektib.png` logo.
- Use the logo palette: coral/pink, lavender, misty teal, and butter yellow, with deep plum ink and purple as dark and primary colors.
- Keep Bricolage Grotesque and Instrument Sans.
- The requested interface direction combines the structural precision and density of Linear with Stripe-like warmth, polished surfaces, and restrained gradient accents. Borrow qualities only; do not use their logos, fonts, or brand assets.

## Evidence on Hand

- `Iskolektib.png` is the supplied iSKOLEKTIB logo.
- Scholarship information is derived from OSA Scholarship and Financial Assistance Section emails.
- The interface labels itself as a demonstration and contains demonstration data; do not present it as an official UP system or invent student outcomes, partner endorsements, or real scholarship results.

## Product Principles

- Make scholarship eligibility and deadlines easy to scan.
- Keep application status and next steps visible to students.
- Support staff listing and application-review work with clear, efficient views.
- Distinguish demonstration records from official opportunities and decisions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, clear keyboard focus, touch targets at least 40px high, and responsive layouts that work down to phone width.
